Billing questions

When do my free reels reset?

On the 1st of every month: 3 Clip → Reel and 1 long-form batch.

What counts as a long-form batch?

Every reel you make from one long video. Finding and previewing the reels in a long video is always free; the batch is used when the first reel from it is made.

How do I cancel Premium?

In the editor, open Settings → Plan and billing → Manage or cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, then it switches to Free. Nothing you made goes anywhere.

What happens to my settings if I stop paying?

Your Premium choices are saved and come back if you upgrade again. Reels you already made are yours to keep.

Who handles payment?

Lemon Squeezy runs checkout and sends your receipts. Reel Studio never sees your card.
