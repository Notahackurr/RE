Questions

Is Reel Studio cheaper than Opus Clip?

Reel Studio Premium is $10 a month or $100 once, with no limit on minutes. Opus Clip's paid plans are $15 a month (150 upload minutes) and $29 a month (300 upload minutes), where one credit is one minute of uploaded video. Both have a free plan.

Does Reel Studio upload my videos like Opus Clip?

No. Reel Studio edits in your browser, on your device, and never uploads your videos. Opus Clip processes your video on its servers.

Is Reel Studio AI like Opus Clip?

Nothing Reel Studio makes is AI-generated: your words, real footage, original music, and every edit follows rules you can see and undo. It uses an open-source speech model on your device to write captions. Opus Clip uses AI to choose clips and score them.
