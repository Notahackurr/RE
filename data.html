Your data

Your videos stay on your device

Reel Studio edits in your browser. Your videos are never uploaded to us or anyone else. This page lists everything that does leave your device, in plain words.

Stays on your device

  • Your videos. They're read, edited and saved by your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Your library. Clips, photos and songs you add are saved in your browser's storage on this device.
  • Your settings and monthly free count. Saved in your browser.
  • The transcript. Captions are written by a speech model running on your device.

Downloaded to your device

  • The editor itself from reeleditapp.com, plus built-in stock clips and music when they're used.
  • The speech model (Whisper, about 80 to 250 MB) and a small sentence model that helps match clips to what you say, from Hugging Face, once, then kept in your browser.
  • Code libraries from jsDelivr, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, these services see your IP address.

Sent from your device, only when you use these

  • Your account (once accounts open): your email address and password, to sign you in and keep your plan on every device. Handled by Supabase.
  • Premium checkout: Lemon Squeezy handles payment and receipts. Reel Studio never sees your card details.
  • A count of reels made: when a reel finishes, 1 is added to a public total shown on the home page. Nothing goes with it: no video, no words, no name, no device ID.
  • Free stock search (if switched on in Settings): a few search words, like “coffee” or “city at night”, are sent to Reel Studio's server and Pexels to find matching clips. Never your video, and never your full transcript.

What Reel Studio doesn't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Your videos, voice and transcript are never used to train anything.

Deleting things

  • Settings → Data and storage removes your clips, your music, or your settings from this device.
  • Settings → Account → Delete my account removes your account. Cancel any subscription first, with Manage in Plan and billing.
