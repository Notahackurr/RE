Privacy

Privacy policy

Reel Studio edits your videos in your browser. Your videos are never uploaded to us. This policy covers the little that does leave your device, who handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. The plain-language version is on Your data.

Who we are

Reel Studio (reeleditapp.com) is run by J.P. Gentry, based in Indiana, USA ("we", "us"). Contact: reelstudioeditor@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your videos, transcripts and finished reels. They're read, edited and saved by your browser. We never receive them.
  • Your library (clips, photos and songs you add), your settings and your free-plan count, saved in your browser's storage.

What we collect, and why

  • Account details, if you create an account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider). Used to sign you in and keep your plan on every device.
  • Purchase records, if you buy Premium: your plan, its status and renewal dates, the order and license key, and the email you bought with, as reported to us by Lemon Squeezy. Used to unlock Premium and handle refunds. We never see your card details.
  • A count of reels made. When a reel finishes, 1 is added to a public total. Nothing else goes with it: no video, words, name or device ID.
  • Free stock searches, if switched on in Settings: a few search words (like "coffee" or "city at night") are sent to our server and to Pexels to find matching clips. Never your video and never your full transcript.
  • Messages you send us by email, to answer them.

We don't run analytics or tracking, don't show ads, don't sell or share your information for advertising, and never use your videos, voice or words to train anything.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in emails and purchase records.
  • Lemon Squeezy: checkout, payments, taxes and receipts. They are the seller of record for Premium, and their own privacy policy covers the payment details you give them.
  • Pexels: free stock search results.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts, jsDelivr (code libraries), Hugging Face (the speech model, downloaded once) and the image host for blog pictures: like any website request, these see your IP address and browser details when your browser loads files from them.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The editor saves your settings, library and sign-in session in your browser's own storage so it works the next time you open it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for your plan, refunds and tax and accounting rules.
  • Emails: as long as needed to help you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the editor: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Cancel any subscription first.
  • Remove everything stored on your device: Settings → Data and storage.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have further rights, and we'll honor them.

Children

Reel Studio isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date below. Big changes will be pointed out in the editor.
