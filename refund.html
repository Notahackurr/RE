Refunds

Refund policy

15-day money-back on Premium, no questions asked.

Lifetime plan ($100 once)

If Reel Studio isn't for you, ask for a refund within 15 days of buying and you get the full $100 back.

Monthly plan ($10 a month)

Your first monthly payment is covered the same way: ask within 15 days of it and it's refunded. After that, monthly payments aren't refunded, but you can cancel anytime and you won't be charged again.

To cancel: in the editor, open Settings → Plan and billing → Manage or cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, then it switches to Free. Nothing you made goes anywhere.

How to ask for a refund

  • Reply to your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, who handle checkout for Reel Studio, or
  • write to reelstudioeditor@gmail.com with the email you bought with.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. Once a refund goes through, your account switches back to the Free plan. Reels you already made stay yours.
