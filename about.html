About

Hi, I'm J.P.

I coach fitness online, and my reels are me talking to a camera in a budget home gym: one take, and a clap whenever I flub a line so I can say it again.

Editing each one took longer than filming it, and the AI tools I tried turned my reels into the same flashy, captions-bouncing stuff everyone else posts.

So I built Reel Studio to edit the way I would by hand: cut the flubs and dead air, caption what I actually said, add real footage and music, and stop there.

J.P. Gentry · Online fitness coach. Edits his own reels with Reel Studio.
