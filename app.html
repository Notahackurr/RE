    Install Reel Studio

    1. On iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    2. On a computer: use the install icon in the address bar (Chrome or Edge), or File → Add to Dock in Safari.
    3. It opens in its own window, like any app.

    Tips from EDDIE

    • Retakes: clap once right after a flub and say the line again. The flub and the clap are cut.
    • Clip → Reel is for a single take. Long form → Reels finds the best stretches of a long video and opens each with its strongest line.
    • Your own clips (Library) are matched first. Describe what they show and the words you'd say over them.
    • After a reel is made, every edit EDDIE made is listed. Tap to watch it, remove it, or add your own, then Update reel.
    • Your videos never leave this device. Settings, clips and tracks are saved in this browser.
