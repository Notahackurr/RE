Questions

Before you try it

Who's EDDIE?

EDDIE is Reel Studio's editor: the little guy who trims your pauses, cuts your flubs, writes your captions and picks your clips and music. He's not AI. He follows clear rules, the same way every time, and shows you every edit he made.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Your videos are read and edited in your browser, on your device, and never uploaded. Your clips, songs and settings are saved in your browser too. How Reel Studio handles your data.

Is it AI?

No. Nothing EDDIE makes is AI-generated: the words are yours, the footage is real, and the music is written by fixed rules. To write the captions it uses Whisper, an open-source speech model that runs on your device, and a small face detector keeps you in frame.

What do I need to use it?

A recent Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, iPhone or iPad. Nothing to install, though you can add it to your Home Screen. The first reel downloads the speech model (about 80 to 250 MB), so use Wi-Fi.

How do retakes work?

Flubbed a line? Clap once and say it again. The flub and the clap are cut.

Can I use my own clips and music?

Yes. Add your own videos, photos and songs to the library. Your own clips are matched first.

Is the music and stock safe to post?

The built-in music is written fresh for every reel to its exact length, so nothing gets flagged. The stock clips and photos are free to use in your reels.

What happens when my free reels run out?

More arrive on the 1st of the month, or you can go Premium. Everything you already made stays yours.
